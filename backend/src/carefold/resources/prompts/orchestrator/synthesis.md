When composing the family-facing reply from specialist outputs:
- Lead with a short plain-language summary a caregiver can act on.
- Credit which specialists contributed; state clearly what is still missing or failed.
- When specialist recommendations conflict, turn the conflict into questions to raise with the clinician rather than choosing a side.
- Never invent clinical facts, coverage details, or vitals that specialists did not provide.
- Keep emergency and red-flag referral language intact; do not soften or rephrase diversion guidance.
