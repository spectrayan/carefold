# Vascular Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for vascular medicine, vein clinic, and vascular surgery consultations.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect all recent diagnostic reports: ABI, arterial duplex, venous duplex, CT angiography, MR angiography, or venography.
- Bring a written list of current medications, including anticoagulants, antiplatelets, statins, and any supplements.
- Prepare your claudication walking-distance log (if applicable) covering the past 7-14 days.
- Prepare your leg swelling log, noting timing (morning vs evening), symmetry, and triggers.
- Note any skin changes around the ankles, feet, or along visible veins, and photograph them if useful.
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Peripheral Arterial Disease (PAD) & Claudication
- What is my ABI value, and how does it compare to my previous reading?
- Based on my walking-distance log, has my claudication improved or worsened?
- Do I need an arterial duplex or CT angiography, and what would it add?
- What exercise program or supervised walking plan do you recommend?
- Are there signs that my PAD is progressing to critical limb ischemia?

### Venous Insufficiency & Varicose Veins
- What do my venous duplex results show about my valve function?
- Which compression class and length are appropriate for me, and how should I wear them?
- Are endovenous ablation, sclerotherapy, or stripping options in my case?
- What skin care routine should I follow to prevent ulcers?

### Post-DVT Follow-Up
- How long do I need to remain on anticoagulation, and what determines the duration?
- What signs of post-thrombotic syndrome should I watch for?
- Do I need follow-up imaging, and if so, when?
- What travel and immobility precautions should I follow?

### Leg Swelling & Edema
- Is my swelling likely vascular, lymphatic, cardiac, or medication-related?
- Should I be evaluated for lymphedema or venous insufficiency?
- What elevation, compression, and activity routine do you recommend?

### Procedures & Recovery
- What are the risks and expected recovery time for the proposed procedure?
- What medications should I stop before the procedure, and when?
- What warning signs after the procedure should prompt me to call or go to the ER?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in, provide insurance and medication list, and mention if you are on anticoagulation.
2. **Pre-visit**: Ask the nurse to record your current medications and any recent vital signs.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top concern, then move through your prioritized questions.
4. **Diagnostics**: Ask whether any imaging or ABI testing will be done today or scheduled separately.
5. **Wrap-up**: Confirm the follow-up interval, any medication changes in writing, and who to call for questions.
