# Claudication Walking-Distance Log Template

Standardized walking-distance tracking worksheet for patients with suspected or confirmed peripheral arterial disease (PAD).

## Purpose
Claudication distance is one of the most useful indicators of PAD severity and progression. A structured log helps your vascular specialist assess changes over time and decide whether further testing or treatment is needed.

## How to Record
- Walk on a flat, familiar route at your usual pace.
- Note the distance (blocks, meters, or time) at which you first feel cramping, aching, or heaviness.
- Note the location of the symptom (calf, thigh, buttock, or foot).
- Note how long you need to rest before the symptom resolves.
- Record each walk on the same or a similar route for comparability.

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 14-day log.
- A note of any change in your walking ability compared to 3 months ago.
- Any episodes of pain at rest, at night, or waking you from sleep.
