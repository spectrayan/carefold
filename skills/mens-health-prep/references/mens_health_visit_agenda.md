# Men's Health Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for men's health and urology visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect recent lab results (PSA, lipid panel, fasting glucose, A1C, testosterone).
- Bring a written list of current medications and supplements.
- Prepare your IPSS urinary symptom log if you have urinary concerns.
- Prepare your cardiovascular/metabolic log (blood pressure readings, glucose logs).
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Preventive Screening & Urology
- When should I start PSA screening, and what are the risks and benefits?
- Do I need a digital rectal exam (DRE) based on my risk factors?
- How often should I perform a testicular self-exam?
- What urinary symptoms should I track and discuss?

### Cardiovascular & Metabolic Health
- What are my blood pressure, cholesterol, and glucose targets?
- Do I need medication for my blood pressure or cholesterol?
- What lifestyle changes can reduce my cardiovascular risk?

### Sexual Health & Hormones
- Are my testosterone levels normal for my age?
- What treatment options are available for erectile dysfunction?
- What are the risks and benefits of testosterone replacement therapy (TRT)?

### Lifestyle & Prevention
- What diet and exercise plan do you recommend for my health goals?
- How does alcohol or smoking affect my urological and cardiovascular health?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in and mention if you are experiencing any acute symptoms (e.g., inability to urinate).
2. **Pre-visit**: Provide your symptom logs and medication list to the nurse.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top concern, then move through your prioritized questions.
4. **Wrap-up**: Confirm follow-up intervals, screening schedules, and warning signs.
