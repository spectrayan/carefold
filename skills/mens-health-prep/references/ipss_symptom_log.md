# International Prostate Symptom Score (IPSS) Log Template

Standardized tracking worksheet for patients with lower urinary tract symptoms (LUTS).

## Purpose
The IPSS is a validated questionnaire used to assess the severity of urinary symptoms and track response to treatment. A structured log helps your urologist understand your symptom pattern.

## How to Record
- Answer the 7 IPSS questions based on your symptoms over the past month.
- Rate each symptom from 0 (not at all) to 5 (almost always).
- Add the scores for a total (0-35).
- Note how often you wake up at night to urinate (nocturia).
