# Cardiovascular & Metabolic Log Template

Tracking worksheet for patients monitoring blood pressure, lipids, glucose, and other metabolic markers.

## Purpose
Cardiovascular and metabolic health are closely linked to urological and hormonal health. A structured log helps your provider assess your risk and tailor treatment.

## How to Record
- Measure blood pressure at the same time each day (rested, seated, arm supported).
- Log fasting blood glucose (if diabetic or prediabetic).
- Note any changes in weight, diet, or exercise.
- Note any medications for blood pressure, cholesterol, or hormones.

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 7-day log.
- A list of current medications and supplements.
- Any recent lab results (lipid panel, A1C, testosterone, etc.).
