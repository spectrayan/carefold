# Nutrition Red Flag Warning Protocol

Clinical decision thresholds to distinguish routine dietary clinic discussion topics from acute or life-threatening emergencies.

## Immediate 911 / Emergency Room Red Flags
Stop using this application and call 911 immediately if you experience:
1. **Severe Allergic Reaction (Anaphylaxis)**: Swelling of the face, lips, tongue, or throat, difficulty breathing, hives, or a drop in blood pressure after eating a specific food.
2. **Severe Dehydration or Malnutrition**: Confusion, rapid heartbeat, fainting, inability to keep fluids down for more than 24 hours, or extreme weakness.
3. **Refeeding Syndrome Risk**: (Especially in severe malnutrition or prolonged fasting) Sudden weakness, confusion, heart palpitations, or muscle cramping after starting to eat again.
4. **Uncontrolled Vomiting or Diarrhea**: Vomiting blood, black tarry stools, or severe diarrhea that does not stop and leads to dizziness or fainting.
5. **Eating Disorder Medical Emergency**: Severe chest pain, fainting, or extreme weakness in the context of severe dietary restriction or purging.

## Urgent Clinic Contact Signs (Call Dietitian/Physician Within 24 Hours)
Contact your dietitian's office or on-call physician if you notice:
- Rapid unintentional weight loss or gain.
- New or worsening inability to swallow solid foods.
- Severe abdominal pain, bloating, or changes in bowel habits that are not improving.
- Signs of vitamin or mineral deficiency (e.g., severe fatigue, mouth ulcers, hair loss).
- Extreme difficulty sticking to a prescribed diet due to side effects.
