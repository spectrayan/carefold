# Macronutrient Tracking Log Template

Daily tracking worksheet for patients monitoring protein, fiber, carbohydrates, fats, and hydration.

## Purpose
Tracking macronutrients helps you and your dietitian see if you are meeting your goals and adjust your plan for better energy, satiety, and symptom control.

## How to Record
- Use a food scale or measuring cups for accuracy.
- Track protein (g), fiber (g), carbohydrates (g), and fats (g) if requested by your dietitian.
- Track water and other fluids (in ounces or liters).
- Note any supplements or vitamins taken.
