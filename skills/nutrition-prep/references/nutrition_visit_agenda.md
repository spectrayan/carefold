# Nutrition Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for nutrition and dietitian visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect recent lab reports (e.g., cholesterol, blood glucose, HbA1c, vitamin levels, kidney function).
- Bring a written list of current medications and dietary supplements.
- Prepare your 7-day food, fluid, and GI symptom journal.
- Note your current dietary goals (e.g., weight loss, symptom management, better energy).
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### General Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT)
- What are the most impactful dietary changes I can make for my condition?
- How quickly should I expect to see changes in my symptoms or lab results?
- Do I need to see a specialist dietitian (e.g., for diabetes, renal, or GI)?

### GI Symptoms & Specialized Diets
- Is a low-FODMAP diet appropriate for me, and how do I start it safely?
- How do I identify food triggers without eliminating too many nutrients?
- Do I need to test for food allergies or intolerances?

### Macronutrients & Lifestyle
- What are realistic protein, fiber, and hydration targets for me?
- How can I adapt these goals to my cooking skills, budget, and schedule?
- What role do supplements (vitamins, minerals, probiotics) play in my plan?

### Diabetes & Metabolic Health
- How can I balance my carbohydrate intake to manage my blood sugar?
- What is a realistic target for my A1C or fasting glucose through diet?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in, provide insurance and medication list.
2. **Pre-visit**: Mention any recent lab work or symptom changes.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top dietary challenge or symptom.
4. **Wrap-up**: Confirm the next steps, meal planning goals, and who to call for questions.
