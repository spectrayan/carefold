# Food & Symptom Log Template

Standardized 7-day tracking worksheet for patients to identify relationships between food, fluid, and gastrointestinal symptoms.

## Purpose
Tracking what you eat and how you feel helps your dietitian identify potential food triggers, assess nutritional adequacy, and tailor a dietary plan to your needs.

## How to Record
- Record meals, snacks, and fluids as soon as possible after eating.
- Note portion sizes (e.g., 1 cup, 1 palm-sized).
- Note GI symptoms (bloating, gas, pain, diarrhea, constipation) and when they occur (e.g., 30 min after eating).
- Rate symptom severity on a 0-10 scale.
- Note any stress, sleep issues, or activity around the meal.
