# Menopause & Perimenopause Symptom Tracker

Tracking worksheet for patients experiencing perimenopausal or menopausal symptoms.

## Purpose
Tracking symptoms over time helps your provider assess the impact of menopause and tailor treatment options.

## How to Record
- Log the frequency and severity of hot flashes and night sweats.
- Note sleep quality and any disruption.
- Note mood changes, anxiety, or brain fog.
- Note any changes in menstrual cycles (if still having periods).
- Note any urinary or vaginal symptoms.
