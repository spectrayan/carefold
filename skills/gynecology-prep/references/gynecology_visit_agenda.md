# Gynecology Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for women's health and gynecology visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect recent test results (Pap smear, HPV test, mammogram, blood work).
- Bring a written list of current medications, including hormonal therapies and supplements.
- Prepare your menstrual and pelvic symptom log covering the past 2-3 cycles.
- Prepare your menopause symptom tracker if applicable.
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Preventive Screening & Well-Woman Exam
- When am I due for my next Pap smear and HPV test?
- Do I need a mammogram or bone density scan based on my risk factors?
- What vaccinations do I need (e.g., HPV, Tdap)?
- What lifestyle changes can support my reproductive health?

### Menstrual & Pelvic Symptoms
- What could be causing my irregular periods or severe cramps?
- Do I need an ultrasound or blood tests to investigate my symptoms?
- What treatment options are available for my pain or heavy bleeding?
- How can I track my cycle more effectively?

### Fertility & Family Planning
- What initial tests should my partner and I consider?
- What lifestyle factors could affect our fertility?
- What are the timelines and success rates for different fertility treatments?
- What are the risks and benefits of egg freezing?

### Menopause & Perimenopause
- How can I manage hot flashes and sleep disruption?
- Is hormone therapy right for me, and what are the risks?
- What lifestyle changes can help with mood and bone health?
- How often should I have follow-up appointments?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in and mention if you are experiencing any acute symptoms.
2. **Pre-visit**: Provide your symptom logs and medication list to the nurse.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top concern, then move through your prioritized questions.
4. **Wrap-up**: Confirm follow-up intervals, screening schedules, and warning signs.
