# Menstrual & Pelvic Symptom Log Template

Standardized tracking worksheet for patients with menstrual or pelvic concerns.

## Purpose
A clear log of cycle patterns, flow, and pain helps your gynecologist identify potential causes and tailor treatment.

## How to Record
- Note the first and last day of your period.
- Rate the flow (light, moderate, heavy, very heavy).
- Note the severity of pelvic pain or cramps (0-10 scale).
- Note any associated symptoms (bloating, mood changes, headaches).
- Note any pain during intercourse or bowel movements.
