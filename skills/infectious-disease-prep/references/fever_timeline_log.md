# Fever & Symptom Timeline Log Template

Standardized tracking worksheet for patients with persistent fever or suspected infection.

## Purpose
A clear timeline helps your doctor identify patterns, determine the source of infection, and know which treatments have or haven't worked.

## How to Record
- Take your temperature at the same times each day (e.g., morning and evening).
- Note the exact time and method (oral, ear, forehead).
- Record symptoms (cough, sore throat, rash, GI issues, joint pain).
- Note when you take fever-reducing medication (e.g., paracetamol, ibuprofen) and if it helped.
- Note any recent antibiotic use.

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 14-day log.
- A list of all medications taken (including antibiotics and antipyretics).
- Photos of any rash (taken in good lighting).
