# Travel & Exposure History Log Template

Tracking worksheet for patients who have recently traveled or been exposed to infectious diseases.

## Purpose
Travel history is critical for diagnosing infections. A structured log helps your doctor know exactly where you went, what you did, and what you might have been exposed to.

## How to Record
- List all destinations and dates of travel (including layovers).
- Note the type of accommodation (hotel, camping, rural).
- Note any insect bites (mosquitoes, ticks), animal contact, or freshwater exposure.
- Note any food or water sources that may have been unsafe.
- Note any vaccinations or malaria prophylaxis taken before travel.
