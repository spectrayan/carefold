# Infectious Disease Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for infectious disease and travel medicine visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect all recent lab reports: CBC, blood cultures, PCR tests, serology, and imaging.
- Bring a written list of current medications, especially all antibiotics taken in the last 6 months.
- Prepare your fever and symptom timeline (dates, temperatures, symptoms).
- Prepare your travel history log (destinations, dates, exposures, vaccinations).
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Fever & Symptom Workup
- What tests are you ordering to find the cause of my fever?
- How long should I expect to wait for the results?
- What should I do if my fever gets worse before my next appointment?

### Antibiotic History
- Do I need a different antibiotic, or is this one not working?
- Are there side effects I should watch out for?
- How can I prevent antibiotic resistance in the future?

### Travel & Exposure
- Do I need post-travel testing for malaria, dengue, or other diseases?
- What vaccinations do I need for my upcoming trip?
- How do I prevent mosquito or tick bites?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in and mention if you have a contagious symptom (cough, rash).
2. **Pre-visit**: Provide your fever log and travel history to the nurse.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top symptom and your biggest worry.
4. **Wrap-up**: Confirm test results timeline, isolation rules, and who to call if symptoms worsen.
