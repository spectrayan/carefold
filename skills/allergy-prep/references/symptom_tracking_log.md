# Symptom & Trigger Tracking Log Template

Standardized tracking worksheet for patients with suspected or known allergies.

## Purpose
Identifying patterns between exposures and symptoms is key to an accurate allergy diagnosis. A structured log helps your allergist pinpoint the exact triggers and assess the severity of your reactions.

## How to Record
- Record the date and time of the reaction.
- Note the suspected trigger (food, medication, insect sting, environmental).
- Describe the symptoms (hives, itching, swelling, wheezing, GI issues).
- Rate the severity on a 0-10 scale.
- Note any medications taken (e.g., antihistamines) and if they helped.
