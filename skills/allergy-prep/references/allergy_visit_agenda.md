# Allergy Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for allergy and immunology visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect all recent lab reports (IgE blood tests, skin-prick test results, pulmonary function tests).
- Bring a written list of current medications (especially antihistamines, inhalers, and epinephrine auto-injectors).
- Prepare your symptom and trigger log (dates, times, suspected allergens, severity).
- Bring your anaphylaxis action plan if you have one.
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Testing & Diagnosis
- What tests are you ordering today (skin-prick, blood tests, patch tests)?
- How long will it take to get the results?
- What are the risks of these tests?

### Testing Prep & Medication Holds
- **Crucial**: What are the exact instructions for holding my antihistamines or other medications before my skin-prick test? (Confirm with the clinic, do not change meds without their approval).
- What should I do if I accidentally take my antihistamine before the test?

### Treatment & Management
- Am I a candidate for immunotherapy (allergy shots or drops)?
- What is the best rescue medication plan for my allergic reactions?
- How do I properly use an epinephrine auto-injector?
- What avoidance strategies do you recommend for my home and workplace?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in, provide insurance, and mention if you are currently having an allergic reaction.
2. **Pre-visit**: Provide your symptom log and medication list to the nurse.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your most severe reaction or biggest concern.
4. **Wrap-up**: Confirm the follow-up interval, test results timeline, and who to call if symptoms worsen.
