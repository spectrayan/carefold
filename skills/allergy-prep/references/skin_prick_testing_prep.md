# Skin-Prick Testing Preparation Checklist

Educational checklist to help you prepare for a skin-prick allergy test and communicate effectively with your allergist's clinic.

## Critical Safety Boundary
**Do not stop, start, or change any of your medications without explicit instructions from your allergist's clinic.** The information below is educational and meant to help you ask the right questions. Every clinic has different protocols.

## Questions to Ask Your Allergist's Clinic Before Testing

- **Antihistamines**: Which specific antihistamines do I need to hold before the test? What is the exact hour window (e.g., 3 days, 7 days)?
- **Other Medications**: Do I need to hold any other medications (e.g., certain antidepressants, acid reducers)?
- **Asthma Inhalers**: Should I continue my asthma medications as usual?
- **Food/Drink**: Should I avoid caffeine or specific foods on the day of the test?
- **Skin Preparation**: Should I avoid lotions or creams on my back or arms on the day of the test?
- **What if I forget?**: What should I do if I accidentally take a medication I was supposed to hold?

## What to Bring to the Test
- Your medication list (including over-the-counter drugs).
- Your epinephrine auto-injector (if prescribed).
- Comfortable clothing that allows access to your back or inner arms.
- A list of your questions for the allergist.

## After the Test
- Expect some itching and redness at the test sites (this is normal).
- Ask how long you need to stay in the clinic for observation.
- Ask what to do if a delayed reaction occurs at home.
