# Oral Health Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for dental, periodontal, and oral surgery visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect all recent diagnostic reports: X-rays, periodontal charting, CBCT, and any specialist referrals.
- Bring a written list of current medications, including antibiotics, anticoagulants, and pain relievers.
- Prepare your daily oral care log covering the past 7-14 days.
- Prepare your symptom tracking log, noting pain location, timing, and triggers.
- Note any bleeding, swelling, or sensitivity patterns, and photograph visible changes if useful.
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Routine Check-Up & Prevention
- What is my current caries and periodontal risk level?
- How often should I have check-ups and cleanings?
- What changes should I make to my daily brushing and flossing routine?
- Do I need fluoride treatment or sealants?

### Gum Health & Periodontal Disease
- What do my periodontal charting numbers show, and how do they compare to last time?
- Do I need scaling and root planing, or a referral to a periodontist?
- What signs of gum disease progression should I watch for at home?
- How can I improve my home care for my gums?

### Tooth Pain & Restorative Needs
- What is causing my tooth pain, and what are the treatment options?
- Do I need a filling, crown, root canal, or extraction?
- What is the expected lifespan of the proposed restoration?
- What aftercare and warning signs should I watch for?

### Extractions & Oral Surgery
- What are the risks and expected recovery time for the extraction?
- What medications should I stop before the procedure, and when?
- What warning signs after the procedure should prompt me to call or go to the ER?
- What foods and activities should I avoid during recovery?

### Cosmetic & Orthodontic Options
- What are the options for whitening, veneers, or alignment?
- What are the risks, costs, and maintenance requirements?
- How long will the results last?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in, provide insurance and medication list, and mention any current pain, swelling, or bleeding.
2. **Pre-visit**: Ask about updated X-rays or periodontal charting, and mention any changes since the last visit.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top concern, then move through your prioritized questions.
4. **Diagnostics**: Ask whether any imaging or tests will be done today or scheduled separately.
5. **Wrap-up**: Confirm the follow-up interval, aftercare instructions, and who to call for questions.
