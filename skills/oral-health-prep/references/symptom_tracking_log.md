# Symptom Tracking Log Template

Tracking worksheet for patients with tooth pain, gum swelling, jaw discomfort, or other oral symptoms.

## Purpose
The location, timing, and triggers of oral symptoms are key clues for your dentist. A structured log helps distinguish between routine sensitivity and something that may need urgent attention.

## How to Record
- Note the location (specific tooth, gum area, jaw, or diffuse).
- Note the timing (morning, after eating, at night, constant).
- Note the triggers (hot, cold, sweet, chewing, pressure).
- Rate the pain on a 0-10 scale.
- Note any associated symptoms (swelling, fever, bad taste, bleeding).

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 14-day log.
- Photos of any visible swelling or gum changes.
- A list of any over-the-counter pain relievers used.
