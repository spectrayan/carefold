# Daily Oral Care Log Template

Standardized daily worksheet for tracking brushing, flossing, mouthwash use, and any bleeding or sensitivity during routine care.

## Purpose
Consistent daily oral care is the foundation of dental health. A structured log helps you notice bleeding, sensitivity, or changes that may need professional attention, and gives your dentist a clear picture of your habits between visits.

## How to Record
- Brush for two minutes, twice a day, ideally with a fluoride toothpaste.
- Floss or use interdental brushes once a day.
- Note any bleeding during or after brushing or flossing.
- Note any sensitivity to hot, cold, sweet, or acidic foods.
- Note any mouthwash or additional products used.

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 14-day log.
- Photos of any visible gum changes or sores, if useful.
- A list of current oral care products (toothpaste, mouthwash, etc.).
