# Lifestyle & Metabolic Log Template

Tracking worksheet for patients monitoring weight, waist circumference, and lifestyle factors.

## Purpose
Lifestyle factors (diet, exercise, sleep, stress) play a crucial role in metabolic health. A structured log helps your provider understand your daily habits and tailor recommendations.

## How to Record
- Measure weight and waist circumference at the same time each week (e.g., morning, empty stomach).
- Note your daily exercise (type and duration).
- Note any changes in diet (e.g., carbohydrate intake, processed foods).
- Note sleep quality and stress levels.
