# CGM & Glucose Log Template

Standardized tracking worksheet for patients monitoring blood glucose and CGM trends.

## Purpose
Tracking glucose patterns helps your provider adjust medications, tailor dietary recommendations, and assess the effectiveness of your metabolic plan.

## How to Record
- Log your fasting blood glucose every morning before eating or drinking.
- Note your CGM average glucose and Time in Range (TIR) if using a CGM.
- Record notable post-meal spikes (time and value).
- Log any hypoglycemic events (symptoms and treatment).
- Note any changes in medication, diet, or exercise.
