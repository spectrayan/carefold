# Metabolic Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for metabolic health and endocrinology visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect recent lab results (HbA1c, fasting glucose, fasting insulin, lipid panel, kidney function).
- Bring a written list of current medications and supplements.
- Prepare your CGM or fasting glucose log covering the past 2-4 weeks.
- Prepare your lifestyle metabolic log (weight, waist circumference, exercise).
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Glycemic Control & Insulin Resistance
- What is my HbA1c trend, and is it improving?
- What are my target blood glucose ranges for fasting and post-meal?
- Do I need additional tests for insulin resistance (e.g., HOMA-IR)?
- What are the options for weight management or metabolic syndrome?

### CGM Interpretation
- What do my CGM trends show about my post-meal spikes?
- Should I adjust my diet or medication timing based on these trends?
- How often should I wear the CGM?

### Lifestyle & Prevention
- What dietary changes will have the biggest impact on my glucose?
- What type and duration of exercise do you recommend?
- How does sleep or stress affect my blood sugar?

### Medication Review
- Are my current diabetes medications at the right dose?
- What are the side effects I should watch out for?
- What should I do if I miss a dose?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in and mention if you have any current symptoms of high or low blood sugar.
2. **Pre-visit**: Provide your glucose logs and medication list to the nurse.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top concern (e.g., high morning readings, frequent lows).
4. **Wrap-up**: Confirm your target ranges, medication changes, and follow-up date.
